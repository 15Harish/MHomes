## Final prompt

Create a polished luxury resort wordmark presentation image. Exact and ONLY text: MHOMES. Six uppercase letters M H O M E S, centered on one line. Wide landscape canvas, aspect ratio 3:1. Solid uniform deep dusk navy background #172638. Solid warm ivory letters #FFF4DD. Exceptionally clean digital typography, pristine smooth edges, no texture, no distress, no speckles, no grain, no glow, no bevel, no shadow. Design an ORIGINAL and distinctive contemporary architectural typeface for this word: broad elegant letter proportions, subtly flared sculptural stems, distinctive deep V cuts in both matching M letters, a low H bridge, a beautifully rounded rectangular O, a projecting architectural top arm on E and a sinuously drawn S. A refined boutique hospitality identity with character, neither plain Arial nor an ornate traditional hotel crest. Tight but clearly separated optical kerning, all letter tops and bottoms aligned, consistent weight, luxurious airy counters. Word occupies 88 percent of canvas width, centered vertically and horizontally, every letter fully inside canvas. This will inspire a giant title emerging behind a warm cream-stone and red-brick resort beneath a blue evening sky, but DO NOT draw any hotel, sky, photograph, mockup, icon, illustration, roof symbol, subtitle, border, or other text. Only one magnificent MHOMES custom wordmark on the uniform navy field. Maximum sharpness, professional brand design.
